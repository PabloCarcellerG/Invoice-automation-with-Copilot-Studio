Microsoft architecture icons
============================

The included Microsoft icons are used only to identify products or document
their interactions in architecture illustrations. They are not the logo of this
accelerator. Original SVG artwork is unmodified, uncropped and proportionally
scaled. Mail and document images are generic architecture symbols, not Outlook
or SharePoint product logos. The Finance icon labels the Dynamics 365 example,
not every possible customer finance system.
The ERP-adapter and human-review symbols are original generic illustrations;
they are not SAP or Microsoft product logos.

Microsoft permits copying, distributing and displaying these icons for
architecture diagrams, training materials and documentation, reserving other
rights. The original applicable license PDFs are included alongside the SVGs.
